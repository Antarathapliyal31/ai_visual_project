2. Release notes: changes since Assignment 3 (Oct 13)

New features

  • The full game loop. Start menu; play; pause with Esc (resume, restart, menu); two endings, Caught (loss) or Dawn after a 3-minute night (win); play again; the best score is kept.
  • HUD. Chaos score (10 per prop knocked over, 40 per one broken), time to dawn, the owner's state and wake meter, a red screen edge while he can see the cat, and rings on the floor where a noise was heard.
  • Noise and the wake meter, on the solver's impulses. Each new contact's impulse becomes loudness on a log scale, scaled by the material pair (the cat's paws are padded), falls off with distance to the owner's head, and fills a meter that drains slowly.
  • The owner AI is in the game (our secondary pillar). Christina's staged state machine and behaviour tree drive an animated owner. He sits up in bed, gets out, walks A* paths round the furniture, looks around where the noise was, kneels to check hiding spots, and chases and catches the cat. His sight is a cone confirmed by line-of-sight rays, so under the bed is a real hiding place and under the desk is not.
  • The coupling proof passes (headless test): the same swat takes him to "sitting up" beside the bed (wake 0.55) and leaves him asleep from the far wall (0.10).
  • From the Assignment 3 feedback: the cat eases into turns (its head leads, its feet step, it leans and its tail swings out), and a bounds check keeps it inside the room.
  • Also merged since Assignment 3: the finished collision layer with its browser proof page (Dharm), the owner model (Christina) and the single-file MVP (Antara).

Bug fixes

  • The cat could jump out of the scene: a jump off the 2 m bookshelf cleared the 2.8 m walls.
  • Five physics bugs from our Oct 22 code review: the sleep toggle was ignored on some worlds, debris ignored the sleep tuning, odd contact limits kept the wrong number of points, the solver's pass count read one low, and the game filtered the cat's contacts before fracture.
  • The game no longer lowers its own render quality while it is still loading.

Known issues

  • Two bugs in the owner state machine itself (its stages never step down, and it moves the owner to the noise) are worked around in the game, not yet fixed in that module.
  • With the painted look on, the game runs at about 50–55 FPS on an Apple M4; the plain render (P) holds 60. Not yet tested in Firefox. No sound yet.
  • Asleep, the owner lies on top of the blanket, and sitting up is shown as kneeling.
  • Under the bed is always safe: he cannot lift the bed skirt.
  • In the single-file MVP the cat barely moves along the floor (cannon-es's stock friction); the game build is not affected.

Stability: 254 headless tests pass. In Chrome: a minute of random input, five restarts, and pausing and resuming, with no errors; scene objects and physics bodies stay constant across restarts.
